1. Priority Ranking Formulation

When automated surveillance flags 100+ violations simultaneously, law-enforcement manpower constraints mandate optimal triage. The Priority Score synthesizes 4 key dimensions:

Priority Score = (ws × Severity) + (wc × Confidence) + (wr × RepeatFactor) + (wq × Quality)
  • Severity (0 - 100): Quantifies lethality risk to human road users. Wrong-way travel and high-speed signal running are classified at the highest hazard tiers.
  • Confidence (0 - 100%): Minimizes officer verification fatigue by surfacing cases with unequivocal detection confidence.
  • Repeat Factor (0 - 125): Escalates chronic violators and vehicles with existing unpaid statutory challans.
  • Evidence Quality (0 - 100): Assesses visual frame resolution and license-plate legibility for unambiguous legal enforcement.

2. Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

The system implements strict human review rather than fully automated fine imposition. Reviewing officers verify bounding box integrity, plate OCR match, and potential emergency vehicle exceptions before digital citation signing.
